## Reel ideas to film

Reels reach people who don't follow you yet. Film vertically on your phone, keep them 7–20 seconds, and put text on screen in the first second.

1. **Hands-only performance.** Film your hands from above playing 15 seconds of something beautiful (Fantasie Impromptu, a jazz standard). On-screen text: *"POV: your piano teacher warms up."*
2. **Before & after.** With permission, a student's first lesson vs. a month later, side by side. Text: *"Week 1 vs. week 6."*
3. **One-minute tip.** One idea, shown at the keys. Hook: *"Stop practicing like this."* Then show the slow-practice fix.
4. **Same song, three styles.** Play a familiar melody classical, then jazz, then pop. Text: *"Which version is your favorite?"* This one invites comments.
5. **The studio.** A slow pan across the studio and keys with soft music. Text: *"Your weekly hour of calm."*
6. **Myth-busting.** Face to camera: *"You don't need talent to learn piano. Here's what you actually need."*

End every Reel caption with a soft call to action: *"Lessons online and in Echo Park. Link in bio."*
